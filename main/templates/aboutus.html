About MindEase

MindEase was created to provide an accessible, compassionate first step for people experiencing mental distress. Our goal is not to replace clinicians, but to augment care and encourage help-seeking when appropriate.

Our mission

We combine empathetic conversational design with best-practice supportive techniques to help people feel heard, reflect and build small, measurable wellbeing habits.
